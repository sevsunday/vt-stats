VT Stats — Ideas & Future Work
================================
Last updated: 2026-05-29

Ordered roughly by value-per-effort. Items that depend on a larger v2
corpus are flagged with [CORPUS GATE]. Items that touch VTSR-T directly

==============================================================
1. Health & Ammo Analytics
==============================================================

PlayerState.health and .ammo are sampled on every UpdateTick but never
aggregated. A single extra pass in the existing UpdateTick loop gets all
of these for free (the loop already fires for positioning; health/ammo
just need to be read alongside position).

NOTE: health and ammo are ABSOLUTE values (engine units, not ratios) per
the proto comment. Normalization by ship max-HP/ammo will be needed for
cross-player and cross-ship comparisons. Max values are not in the proto
directly — derive per player per match as the observed maximum, or look
up from odf.min.json weapon/vehicle entries.

----- Cheap pipeline additions -----

"Time spent low-health"
  Fraction of alive-ticks where health < 25% of the player's observed
  match-maximum for that ship. A "lives dangerously" descriptor.
  Emit as personal.time_low_health_pct (0–1).

"Ammo-out moments"
  Count of distinct episodes where ammo == 0 (debounced across
  consecutive ticks). Measures supply discipline / trigger management.
  Emit as personal.ammo_out_episodes (int).

HP-efficiency
  Damage dealt divided by HP lost. A rough "damage-for-HP" economy.
  Distinct from K/D in that it captures partial exchanges.
  Emit as personal.hp_efficiency (float, null if no damage taken).

----- Medium — genuinely novel skill-adjacent signals -----

Risk/aggression profile
  Mean health at the moment a player's bullet hits an enemy (join
  BulletHit shooter to the PlayerState sample nearest that tick). A
  brawler trades at 30% HP; a careful player only engages near full.
  Emit as personal.mean_health_at_engagement (float, 0–1 normalized).
  Pairs with the distance range fingerprint (Section 2) to build a
  2-axis playstyle descriptor — see Section 3.

Clutch / comeback detection (highlight card candidate)
  A player who dropped below ~10% HP and then survived (HP recovered
  past 50%) or got a kill before dying. Real, legible skill moment.
  "Bullet Sponge" tracks damage absorbed; this is distinct: actual
  survival after near-death. Gates: need health data AND UnitDestroyed
  alignment to know the player wasn't actually killed.

Free servicing / repair inference
  A sharp upward step in HP or ammo between consecutive ticks = a
  service-pad or repair visit, inferred without relying on the noisy
  PickupPowerup event stream. Useful cross-check against pickup data;
  could clean up the "pad visit" counting problem described in
  DATA_DICTIONARY.md §8.

----- Longer horizon -----

Health overlay in the Replay / Timeline player
  Per-player HP line running under the playhead. Turns the replay into
  something you can narrate frame-by-frame ("watch the bar crater at
  tick 4200"). High wow-factor; moderate effort since the timeline
  engine already exists in js/timeline-player.js.

"Death anatomy"
  Reconstruct the last N seconds of HP before each death (burst vs
  attrition). Feeds a per-match "how people died" meta chart: instant
  burst deaths vs drawn-out attrition deaths vs snipes.

[RATING] HP-grounded survivability axis
  The current Survivability radar axis is a proxy (damage-trade ratio
  + K/D composite). With real HP you can measure damage absorbed before
  death and HP-efficiency directly — replacing the proxy with a real
  measurement. This is the ONE health idea worth eventually feeding into
  VTSR-T, but only after:
  (a) the descriptive version ships and looks sane, and
  (b) scripts/validate_elo.py confirms the new axis improves predictive
      accuracy vs the current proxy.
  [CORPUS GATE: ~150+ v2 matches before attempting]


==============================================================
2. Distance & Engagement Range
==============================================================

Source field: BulletHit.distance_to_target (float, world units).
Available only in v2 sessions. Gate any consumer on
match.bullet_hit_distance.with_distance > 0.

Framing note: accuracy-as-a-function-of-distance is too confounded to
be a player STRENGTH signal (weapon choice drives range more than skill),
and the v2 corpus is too small to weapon-normalize it into a VTSR-T
axis. Treat distance as a PLAYSTYLE DESCRIPTOR and META-ANALYSIS input,
not a quality metric. Do NOT add a distance-based VTSR-T axis until
validate_elo.py shows it improves prediction with a large corpus.

----- Worth doing now -----

Per-weapon engagement-range meta (match and corpus-wide)
  For each weapon ODF: mean / p50 / p95 hit distance across all BulletHit
  events. Shows how the community actually deploys each weapon vs what
  the ODF specs imply. Natural fit for the All Matches → Meta tab or the
  docs page weapon table.
  Pipeline: in the bullet_hit loop accumulate per-odf distance lists
  (or running Welford mean + sorted p95 estimate).

Per-player range fingerprint (descriptive, not scored)
  Bucket PvP hits into [0–50m, 50–150m, 150–400m, >400m] per player.
  Render as a small 4-bar sparkline in the Player Leaderboard or profile.
  Frame as "Brawler / Skirmisher / Sniper" flavor — NOT a quality score.
  Emit as personal.distance_buckets: {close, mid, long, extreme} (counts).

Long-range kill chip in the kill feed
  When a kill follows a BulletHit with distance_to_target > 400m,
  tag the kill-feed entry with long_range_kill: true. Renders as a
  flavor chip in the feed (e.g. "⟩400m"). No strength claim.

----- Deferred -----

Distance-weighted accuracy as a VTSR-T axis — DEFERRED INDEFINITELY.
  Weapon choice confound is too strong. Revisit only if weapon-normalized
  hit-rate proves predictive in validate_elo.py with a large corpus.

"The Sniper" highlight card — DEFERRED.
  Same underlying issue. Frame as a flavor note (long-range kill chip)
  rather than an award until the signal is validated.

----- Implementation notes -----

- Normalize by base_to_base_distance for cross-map comparisons
  (200m is "across the map" on Octavia; barely past spawn on Bunker Trip).
  base_to_base_distance is available from the positioning block.
- Filter to PvP-only and shooter_team != victim_team before any analytics.
- v1 sessions always have distance_to_target == 0.0 (field absent in
  legacy proto — protobuf default). The >0 gate is sufficient to skip them.


==============================================================
3. Playstyle Profile (synthesis of distance + health)
==============================================================

Combine the range fingerprint (Section 2) and the risk/aggression metric
(Section 1) into a 2-axis playstyle quadrant per player:

  Axis 1 (X): engagement range  — brawler (short) ↔ sniper (long)
  Axis 2 (Y): aggression        — cautious (high HP at engagement) ↔
                                   reckless (low HP at engagement)

Renders as a small scatterplot on the player profile page (or a
quadrant-badge: "Cautious Sniper" / "Reckless Brawler" etc.). Purely
descriptive, highly shareable, and avoids any validity-of-skill claim.

Both inputs (distance bucket mode + mean_health_at_engagement) are
pipeline-computable in the same loop pass. The rendering is lightweight
(a single Chart.js scatter point per player on the match leaderboard,
or a named label on the profile).

4. Faction Analytics
==============================================================

team1_race / team2_race from the v2 StatHeader are already ground-truthed
(pipeline uses them as authoritative in detect_team_factions() since the
v2 rollout). The missing layer is analytics ON TOP of the faction data.

NOTE: faction balance is a META property, not a player-skill property.
Do NOT bake faction into VTSR-T — it would penalize players for their
assigned faction, not their play.

----- Trivial / useful -----

Faction matchup matrix (All Matches → Meta tab)
  ISDF vs Scion vs Hadean (and mirror matches) win rates + determined-
  match counts. All the data exists: match.winner + match.team_factions.
  Gate behind the existing "≥5 determined matches" em-dash floor per cell.
  Add faction balance over time (by match date) and by map as variants.

----- Medium -----

Per-player faction performance (player profile page)
  Does a player have a higher K/D or VTSR-T delta as Scion vs ISDF?
  The loyalist highlights (isdf_loyalist etc.) already track *frequency*;
  this is faction-conditioned *performance*. A "specialist vs generalist"
  descriptor + per-faction stat split block on the profile would be
  genuinely novel.

Faction heatmap splits on the positioning tab
  Overlay per-faction movement traces with different colors (already have
  faction code per player). Useful on maps with lopsided base geometry.

----- Deferred -----

Faction-aware VTSR-T axis — DEFERRED. Same reasoning as above.
  [CORPUS GATE: reassess when ≥150 v2 matches AND validate_elo.py shows
  faction-conditioning improves win-rate prediction]


==============================================================
5. Positioning & Movement (Phase 4)
==============================================================

Bundle 5A + 5B together — both are positioning visualizations. Users
notice the layout shift once instead of twice.

----- 5A. Heatmap calibration tool (build first) -----

Every map in data/map-registry.json has image_calibration: null, so the
dashboard falls back to terrain_bounds for everyone → most maps are
subtly misaligned. Hand-tuning JSON via file-edit-reload is too slow.

Tasks:
  1. Build calibrate.html (or ?calibrate=1 mode on dashboard).
     - Loads any one match's positioning data + map image.
     - Two draggable corner handles (or 4 sliders) for
       min.x / max.x / min.z / max.z of image_bounds_world.
     - Live re-render of the heatmap on every drag.
     - "Copy JSON" button outputs the calibration block ready
       to paste into data/maps/<key>.json.
  2. build_map_registry.py already preserves the per-map JSON
     calibration across reruns (lines 265-296). No pipeline change needed.

----- 5B. Audit + tune real maps -----

Tasks:
  1. Build tools/heatmap-audit.html: a single-page grid view showing
     every map at thumbnail size with terrain bounds outlined and a
     synthetic heatmap overlaid. One-glance answer to "which maps need
     fixing?".
  2. Hand-calibrate the top N most-played maps using the tool from 5A.
  3. Look for batch patterns: maps screenshotted with the same conventions
     may share the same offset.

----- 5C. Document the calibration workflow -----

In DEVELOPER_GUIDE.md "Map Assets and Overlays":
  - The two-source precedence (image_calibration → terrain_bounds).
  - Why iondriver images often don't match terrain extents.
  - Step-by-step using the calibration tool.
  - Calibration values can be negative (terrain origin is map-center
    for many maps).

Do NOT:
  - Auto-derive image bounds from terrain_bounds. They're often genuinely
    different and any heuristic will be wrong as often as right.
  - Crop/resize the underlying images. Calibrate the projection, not the asset.

----- 5D. Distance-from-Spawn chart fate -----

Recommendation: demote the chart to a per-player drilldown only.
It currently occupies col-lg-7 (60% of a row) for three view modes
plus a smoothing toggle. Lots of real estate for one chart.

Stretch: replace the demoted chart with two sharper cards:
  - "First leave" timeline: horizontal swimlane, 1 row per player, marks
    the tick when each player first crossed R_base. The actually
    interesting story buried in the current chart.
  - "Time near base" stacked bar: one bar per player, segmented by
    in-base vs out-of-base seconds.

Implementation pointers:
  - renderDistanceTimeline() lives at js/positioning-charts.js line 416.
    Existing modes stay; just call from a per-player modal instead.
  - Card removed from index.html ~line 583. Replace with new cards or
    leave the col empty for the heatmap to grow.

  A small "Mines Deployed vs Destroyed" panel on the Combat tab.

  count deployments alongside destructions.

Snipe Feed widget promotion
  Consider promoting top snipers to a sidebar widget on the Combat tab
  if matches trend snipe-heavy.

Career: total_powerup_destructions and total_deployable_kills
  Fall out naturally from existing per-match blocks. Skipped in Phase 3
  to keep career_stats minimal; add when the data proves interesting.

KNOWN_POWERUP_ODFS / KNOWN_DEPLOYABLE_ODFS maintenance
  Re-run scripts/audit_pickup_powerup.mjs whenever a new map/mod ships.
  Look for unfamiliar ODFs with team-zero >= 80%, total >= 5. Apply
  domain knowledge to classify. Do NOT auto-promote based on team-zero
  signal alone — see fball2c.odf precedent in DATA_DICTIONARY.md §8.

PlayerState.speed
  Never read (movement is derived from position deltas instead). Low
  priority, but "average speed while dealing/taking damage" is a cheap
  mobility-flavor stat, and it's a free cross-validation of the
  heatmap activity pipeline if ever needed.
